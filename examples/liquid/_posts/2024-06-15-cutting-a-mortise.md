---
title: Cutting a mortise by hand
tags: [joinery, tools]
category: joinery
---
Mark the walls with a gauge set to the chisel's width, chop from the middle
out, and lever the waste toward you. Stop {{ 2 | plus: 1 }} mm short of the
line and pare to it.

<figure class="note">
  <figcaption>Depth check</figcaption>
  Wrap tape around the chisel at the depth, and stop when the tape meets the wood.
</figure>

Keep the chisel's back toward the line&nbsp;&mdash; the bevel pushes it away
from the cut. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd> to print this page for the
bench.

See also [the note on sharpening]({% post_url 2024-03-02-sharpening-chisels %}).
